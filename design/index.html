What we do

From understanding problems to building products.

  1. Find the right problem to solve. We explore user needs, review existing experiences and test assumptions to help teams decide what matters most—and where to go next.

    • UX Research
    • Usability Testing

  2. Make complex products feel simple. We turn insights into clear interfaces, reusable design systems and interactive prototypes, helping teams align on the experience before development begins.

    • UI Design
    • Design System
    • Prototype

  3. We design for our own teams, too. From the BCT Website and presentation decks to Weekday, our work spans brand communication and tools for everyday work. We also explore AI products through Office Power, bringing AI agents into the workplace.

    • Weekday
    • Office Power - AI product
How we work

Five steps, no surprises

Every project moves through the same rhythm. You see progress every week, not a big reveal in week twelve.

1

Discover

Understand the business, the users and the constraints.

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Business analysis
  • Personas
2

Define

Agree on the problem before we solve it.

  • User flows
  • Information architecture
  • Scope & goals
3

Design

Sketch fast, then refine what works.

  • Wireframes
  • Visual design
  • Interactive prototypes
4

Validate

Put it in front of real people and listen.

  • Usability testing
  • Design review with dev
  • Iteration
5

Deliver

Ship it together, then keep improving.

  • Dev-ready specs
  • Design system updates
  • Design QA
